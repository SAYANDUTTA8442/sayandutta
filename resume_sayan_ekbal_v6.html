ECOT-ERG: Emotion-Cause Chain-of-Thought Framework for Empathetic Response Generation
  • Architected and implemented the ECOT-ERG reasoning framework, integrating emotion prediction, cause identification, chain-of-thought supervision, and T5-based response generation into a unified end-to-end pipeline.
  • Built modular emotion prediction and cause identification components, enabling independent evaluation and ablation of each reasoning stage without retraining the full system.
  • Fine-tuned T5 models with FP16 mixed-precision training; constructed DPO preference pairs from ranked model outputs for empathy alignment.
  • Executed 16 experimental configurations (4-way ablation: CoT supervision × Emotion Prediction × Cause Identification × DPO); evaluated on BERTScore, EASE, Cause-F1, and Relevance-Accuracy.
  • Co-authored ACL ARR May 2026 submission; generated and analyzed qualitative outputs for error analysis and paper discussion.
